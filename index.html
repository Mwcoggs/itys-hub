How it's built

Player projections come from Sleeper's weekly feed, rescored with this league's rules (6-point passing TDs, full PPR, custom kicker and defense scoring). Each week the model starts the highest-projected legal lineup from active roster players; taxi and IR spots are excluded and players on bye score zero.

Availability is applied on top of those projections, refreshed every morning. A player on injured reserve, PUP, the non-football injury list or suspension — whatever keeps him off the field — is a zero for the next four weeks and is discounted by half after that, because a return date is a guess. For the coming week only, Out counts as zero, Doubtful as 40% and Questionable as 90%. Everyone's current absences show up under their team in the rankings.

Power score blends the rest-of-season rating with what teams have actually scored. Results now carry real weight: 50% by week 5 and about 70% by week 10, with recent weeks counting for more than September. Strength of schedule uses full-strength lineups, as described above, while the weekly win chances use each team's actual projection for that week, byes and all. Win chances assume each team's weekly score varies by about 30 points around its projection. Playoff seeding uses wins, then points for, and the bracket runs 4 vs 5 and 3 vs 6 in week 15, with the winners facing seeds 1 and 2 in week 16 and the final in week 17.

Data refreshes every morning from Sleeper. Later-week projections are rougher and don't know about future injuries or trades.
